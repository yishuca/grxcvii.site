03 / Approach

How we build ground

Four working principles
Applied to every commission

01

Survey first

We begin with what is already there — soil, water, wind, footfall and the habits nobody writes down. Nothing is drawn until the site has been read.

02

Draw at 1:1

Every edge, step and seat is tested at the scale of a body before it is specified. If it does not work as a mock-up, it does not work at all.

03

Build with time

A landscape is not finished on opening day. We design the first year, the fifth and the fiftieth — and write the maintenance into the material.

04

Leave room

The best public spaces keep a margin for the unplanned. We resist filling every square metre, and design the gaps as deliberately as the objects.

04 / Studio

Nine people, one table

Rotterdam · Since 2009
Landscape, ecology, engineering

FIELDWORK was founded in 2009 by two landscape architects and a soil scientist who kept meeting on the same muddy sites. The studio has stayed deliberately small — nine people, one long table, a shared library of stones and seed samples.

We work across public realm, water infrastructure and housing, usually alongside architects and engineers, and always with the people who will actually use the place.

“A landscape is the only building that gets better when you stop touching it.”
