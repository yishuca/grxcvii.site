01 — About

A speaker is a door. I just decide where it opens.

Mira Voss is a composer and sound artist working between score, installation and generative systems. Her practice begins with a room — its resonance, its history, the way people move through it — and builds sound that makes that room audible.

Recent work includes scores for documentary and dance, a permanent sound installation in Copenhagen's harbour, and the ongoing Field Notes series of site-specific recordings.
